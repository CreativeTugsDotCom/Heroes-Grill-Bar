ABOUT HEROES

Authentic American Neighborhood Grill & Bar in Berlin, CT.

YOUR NEIGHBORHOOD GRILL & BAR

Heroes Grill & Bar brings together classic comfort food, refreshing drinks, and a welcoming neighborhood atmosphere. Whether you're joining us for a meal, catching up with friends, or enjoying a night of karaoke, there's always a reason to stop by.

Located at 128 Mill Street in Berlin, CT, we pride ourselves on being a genuine local pub where regulars and newcomers alike feel right at home. With sports screens, classic wood and brick finishes, hot burgers, and cold beers, Heroes is your local spot for good times.
